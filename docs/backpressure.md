## OOM-safety claim

`max_queue_depth` is bounded by `queue_size` in every row above (3 rows, queue_size [8, 32], n [500, 5000]). That's the invariant that makes `stream` safe to point at an unbounded source: peak in-memory items are O(queue_size), not O(producer_rate × time).

The rows span more than one `n` ([500, 5000]) at a fixed `queue_size`, which is what makes this table evidence for the `n`-independence of the bound rather than only for its value at one workload size. The general claim is proved where it belongs, over a parameterised table in `tests/test_stream.py` -- `test_stream_metrics_max_depth_bounded_by_queue_size` -- which is host-independent; this table is the measured illustration.
